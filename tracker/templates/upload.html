Upload job spreadsheet

Upload an Excel (.xlsx) or .csv file with one row per job line. The first row should be the column headings.

  • A row with a line becomes a job with that line and a total quantity. Its parts are entered later, when they go on a pallet.
  • A row without a line becomes a one-part job with its part number and quantity.
  • You'll check the columns, combine lines, and review everything before anything is saved.
