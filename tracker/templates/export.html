Export to spreadsheet

Each button downloads a .csv file that opens in Excel. Nothing is changed in the app.

Jobs Parts Pallets and what is on them Activity log
  • Jobs: every job, shipped or not, with quantity, pallets, status, due date, and the dates it was completed, wrapped, and shipped.
  • Parts: every part with its job, quantity, stage, how many are on pallets, taken off, or not yet on a pallet.
  • Pallets: one row for each part on each pallet, with the pallet's zone and status. Mixed pallets include the job.
  • Activity log: everything recorded, oldest first.
