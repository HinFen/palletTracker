About the Pallet + Parts Tracker

The Pallet + Parts Tracker keeps one shared record of the shop's jobs, the parts in them, the pallets those parts sit on, and where each pallet is on the floor, so anyone can check from a phone or computer without walking the floor or asking around.

What it does

  • Tracks jobs, one-part jobs and big jobs with lines, through each stage to shipping.
  • Keeps job pallets and mixed pallets, with what is on each and which zone it is in.
  • Shows the floor as a map of zones, with due-date warnings, search, and a history of every change.
  • Reads the job spreadsheet, exports to Excel, and backs itself up every day.

Built with Python and Flask.
